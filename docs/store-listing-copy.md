## 📖 详细描述 (Description)

### 版本 1：功能导向（推荐）

```markdown
ZuiTi helps you craft high-quality, contextual replies on X (Twitter) in seconds with AI assistance.

🚀 KEY FEATURES

• Multiple AI Providers
  Choose from Gemini, OpenAI (GPT), Claude, or DeepSeek. Use your own API keys or sign in with Google OAuth for Gemini.

• Smart Model Rotation
  Automatically switches between AI models to maximize free quota usage. Never worry about hitting rate limits.

• 8 Reply Styles
  - 💼 Professional: Formal and authoritative tone
  - 😊 Friendly: Warm and approachable
  - 😄 Humorous: Light-hearted and witty
  - 🧠 Insightful: Deep analysis with unique perspectives
  - 💪 Supportive: Encouraging and empathetic
  - 🤔 Questioning: Thought-provoking questions
  - ✨ Concise: Brief and to the point
  - 🎨 Custom: Use your own prompt template

• One-Click Generation
  Click the ⚡ lightning icon next to any reply button. AI reads the tweet context and generates a relevant reply instantly.

• Secure & Private
  - API keys encrypted with AES-GCM and stored locally
  - No data sent to our servers
  - Open source on GitHub


🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY

Your privacy matters. API keys are encrypted and stored only in your browser's local storage. We don't collect analytics or upload your data to our servers.

Tweet content is sent directly to your chosen AI provider (Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, or DeepSeek) to generate replies. See our Privacy Policy for full details.


📖 HOW TO USE

1. Install the extension
2. Click the ZuiTi icon in your toolbar → "Settings"
3. Add your API key:
   - Gemini: Get free API key at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
   - Or use Google OAuth to sign in
   - OpenAI/Claude/DeepSeek: Add your own API keys
4. Visit X (Twitter) and open any tweet
5. Click the reply button to open the reply box
6. Look for the ⚡ lightning icon next to the reply box
7. Click it to open the AI reply generator
8. Select your preferred style
9. AI generates a contextual reply
10. Edit if needed, click "Copy", paste and send


🆓 FREE QUOTA (approximate)

• Gemini: 15 requests/min (1,500/day with free API key)
• DeepSeek: 10 requests/min
• OpenAI/Claude: Depends on your API key plan

The extension tracks usage and automatically rotates models to help you stay within free limits.


⭐ WHY ZUITI?

• Save Time: Generate quality replies in seconds, not minutes
• Maintain Voice: Choose from 8 styles or create custom prompts
• Stay Engaged: Reply to more conversations without burnout
• Learn from AI: See different approaches to responding
• No Vendor Lock-in: Switch between 4 AI providers anytime


🛠️ TECHNICAL DETAILS

• Manifest V3 compliant
• Works on x.com and twitter.com
• Supports Chrome, Edge, Arc, Brave, and other Chromium browsers
• Local-first: All settings stored in your browser
• Open source: https://github.com/innomad-io/zui-ti


💬 SUPPORT & FEEDBACK

Need help or have suggestions?
• GitHub: https://github.com/innomad-io/zui-ti/issues
• X (Twitter): @innomad_io
• Website: https://innomad.io


📜 PRIVACY POLICY

Read our full privacy policy at:
https://innomad-io.github.io/zui-ti/privacy-policy.html


Built with ❤️ for the X community by @innomad_io
```
